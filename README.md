# MindTag 🧠
### A Thought Organizer for Overthinkers

MindTag is an AI-powered thought organizer designed to help people make sense of their thoughts.

Sometimes, our minds feel cluttered with endless thoughts, worries, ideas and emotions. MindTag aims to make this easier by allowing users to write down what's on their mind, understand their thoughts through AI-powered categorisation, and receive helpful suggestions.

## 💡 The Idea Behind MindTag

The idea is simple: instead of keeping everything in your head, put your thoughts into words.

MindTag uses AI to help organise those thoughts and turn mental clutter into something easier to understand and work through.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Thought Capture:** Write down thoughts, concerns, ideas or anything on your mind.
- **AI-Powered Categorisation:** Analyse and organise thoughts into relevant categories.
- **Helpful Suggestions:** Receive AI-generated suggestions based on the thoughts shared.
- **Thought Organisation:** Make scattered thoughts easier to understand and manage.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. Write down what's on your mind.
2. Submit your thoughts for AI analysis.
3. MindTag processes and categorises the input.
4. Receive relevant suggestions to help you understand and approach your thoughts.

It combines technology, creativity and problem-solving to make everyday thought organisation more approachable.
